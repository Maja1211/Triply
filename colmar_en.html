Colmar – A Christmas Fairytale in the Heart of Alsace

Trip Description

Welcome to Colmar – a fairytale town in the heart of Alsace, known for its colorful houses, romantic canals, and magical Christmas atmosphere.

During the winter, Colmar turns into a Christmas wonderland: twinkling lights, mulled wine, festive markets, and music fill every corner of this charming town.

Explore local museums, taste Alsatian wines, and experience French cuisine in cozy bistros. With Triply, your holiday in Colmar will be filled with warmth, joy, and the spirit of Christmas.
